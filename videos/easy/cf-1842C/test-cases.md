# Test-case walkthroughs

For `1 2 2 3 3`, delete the two `2` balls and then the two `3` balls. Four balls disappear, matching the first official sample output.

For `1 2 1 2`, deleting positions one through three leaves one ball, so the answer is three.

For `1 2 3`, no equal endpoints exist. Every ball remains and the answer is zero.
