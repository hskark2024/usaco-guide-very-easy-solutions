# Storyboard and slide outline

Voice: Daniel, a calm male macOS voice for a 9th-grade audience. Each narration paragraph matches one slide.

1. Show buildings of different heights with different price tags.
2. Move a horizontal target-height line and count weighted brick changes.
3. Plot the V-shaped cost of one building.
4. Overlay several V shapes and reveal their convex sum.
5. Animate the `f(mid)` versus `f(mid + 1)` decision.
6. Walk through the official three-building sample and total 120.
7. Shade the useful target range from zero to maximum height.
8. Highlight the `1LL` multiplication and `long long` accumulator.
9. End with the time and space complexity card.
