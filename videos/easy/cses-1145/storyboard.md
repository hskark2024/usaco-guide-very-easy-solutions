# Storyboard and slide outline

Voice: Daniel, a calm male macOS voice for a 9th-grade audience. Each numbered narration paragraph matches one slide.

1. Highlight a valid strictly increasing selection inside a larger array.
2. Show several same-length subsequences and keep only the one with the smallest final value.
3. Arrange cards for lengths one through four, each labeled with its best tail.
4. Drop a new value onto the first card whose tail is at least that value.
5. Move the pointer past all cards and animate a new length card appearing.
6. Walk through `7 3 5 3 6 2 9 8` and end with four cards.
7. Show equal values replacing the same card rather than growing the answer.
8. Display complexity, the invariant, and brute-force verification.
