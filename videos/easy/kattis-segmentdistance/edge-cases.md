# Edge-case checklist

- Proper interior crossing.
- Shared endpoint and T-shaped touch.
- Collinear partial or full overlap.
- Parallel disjoint segments.
- Closest perpendicular landing inside a segment.
- Closest point being an endpoint.
- Reversed segment endpoints.
- One or both segments reduced to points.
- Large coordinates requiring wide exact cross products.
- Answers that round to two decimal places.
