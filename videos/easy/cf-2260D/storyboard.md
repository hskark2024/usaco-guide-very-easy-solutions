# Storyboard and slide outline

Voice: Daniel, a calm male macOS voice for a 9th-grade audience. Each narration paragraph matches one slide.

1. Introduce the sign string and minimax cost.
2. Convert array values into movement between prefix sums.
3. Show why equal neighboring states are forbidden.
4. Divide candidate states into negative, zero, and positive.
5. Display the seven-state number line.
6. Define the minimum-maximum DP state.
7. Animate one transition and its maximum update.
8. Take minima per state and at the finish.
9. Walk through impossible zero cases.
10. Close with exhaustive and maximum-length tests.
