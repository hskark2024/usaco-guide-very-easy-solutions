# Storyboard and slide outline

Voice: Daniel, a calm male macOS voice for a 9th-grade audience. Each narration paragraph matches one slide.

1. Show two horizontal rows of points and one highlighted triangle.
2. Label the base, height two, and area equal to the base length.
3. Animate pairing the outermost points, then moving inward.
4. Split `k` triangles into `x` lower pairs and `k-x` upper pairs.
5. Display the two row-capacity inequalities and the feasible interval.
6. Plot a concave score hill and compare neighboring integer points.
7. Draw the coordinate rows from the second official example.
8. Walk through scores 150 and 200 for one and two triangles.
9. Present the `k_max` formula and the full algorithm pipeline.
10. End with 64-bit safety and complexity.
