# Storyboard and slide outline

Voice: Daniel, a calm male macOS voice for a 9th-grade audience. Each numbered narration paragraph matches one slide.

1. Animate a white tree turning black outward from one starting vertex, with score bubbles.
2. Root the tree and outline a chosen vertex's untouched descendant component.
3. Replace each outlined subtree with a size card and add the cards.
4. Slide the root marker across one highlighted edge instead of rebuilding the tree.
5. Color the child's side blue with `S` vertices and the other side gold with `N-S`.
6. Animate `-S + (N-S)` simplifying to `N-2S`.
7. Show five root score cards for the official example and circle `14`.
8. Animate reverse order for sizes and forward order for rerooting, ending on `O(N)`.
