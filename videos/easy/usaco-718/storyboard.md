# Storyboard and slide outline

Voice: Daniel, a calm male macOS voice for a 9th-grade audience. Each numbered narration paragraph matches one slide.

1. Draw two ordered field columns and several friendly crosswalk candidates.
2. Animate two crossing lines, then show that legal selected endpoint indices rise together.
3. Turn the two columns into a prefix grid from `(0,0)` to `(N,N)`.
4. Highlight the up and left transitions as skipping one newest field.
5. Highlight a friendly diagonal transition and show why it cannot cross earlier pairs.
6. Put the three recurrence candidates side by side and take their maximum.
7. Walk through the reversed sample and reveal five selected crosswalks.
8. Display `O(N²)` time, rolling rows, and exhaustive verification.
