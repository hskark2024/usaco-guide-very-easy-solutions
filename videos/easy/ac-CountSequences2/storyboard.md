# Storyboard and slide outline

Voice: Daniel, a calm male macOS voice for a 9th-grade audience. Each narration paragraph matches one slide.

1. Introduce prescribed frequencies and modular counting.
2. Visualize two ones and two twos in four slots.
3. Add one group of identical values to an existing arrangement.
4. Turn the insertion choices into a product of binomial coefficients.
5. Explain why arbitrary composite moduli break modular division.
6. Build binomial coefficients with Pascal addition.
7. Connect the 5000 bound to one shared precomputation.
8. Show triangular flat-array storage.
9. Walk through counts 4, 2, and 5.
10. Finish with the bijection, complexity, and verification.
