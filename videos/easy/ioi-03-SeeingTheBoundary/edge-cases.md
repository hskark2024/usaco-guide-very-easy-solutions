# Edge-case checklist

- Tangent ray passes exactly through a fence post.
- Tangent ray hits a fence corner.
- Blocked wedge contains no integer post.
- Interval crosses the perimeter numbering cut.
- Several vertices share one viewing direction.
- Multiple rocks have overlapping blocked intervals.
- Observer is close to a side but touches neither fence nor rock.
- Maximum `N`, maximum rock count, and 20 vertices per rock.
