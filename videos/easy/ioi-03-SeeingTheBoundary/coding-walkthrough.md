# C++ coding walkthrough

1. Store points and a positive-denominator `Fraction` type.
2. In `perimeter_hit`, compare side-hit times by cross multiplication.
3. Build the appropriate rational perimeter formula for the hit side.
4. Sort vertex angles and scan cyclic gaps to identify the two tangents.
5. Compare tangent perimeter fractions exactly and unroll the end when needed.
6. Use integer ceiling and floor to form an inclusive post interval.
7. Split wraparound intervals, sort pairs, and merge adjacent or overlapping ranges.
8. Print `4*N` minus the merged length.
