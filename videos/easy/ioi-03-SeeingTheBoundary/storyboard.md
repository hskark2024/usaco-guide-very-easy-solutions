# Storyboard

1. Show the observer, square fence, integer posts, and two rocks.
2. Rotate a ray and shade one rock's continuous blocked wedge.
3. Plot vertex directions on a circle and mark the largest outside gap.
4. Extend the two tangent rays to the square boundary.
5. Unroll the boundary into positions `0..4N`.
6. Demonstrate `ceil(start)` and `floor(end)`, including an exact endpoint post.
7. Split a wraparound interval and merge all rock intervals.
8. Subtract blocked union length from `4N` and review complexity.
