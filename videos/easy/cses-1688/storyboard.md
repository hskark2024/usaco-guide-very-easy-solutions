# Storyboard and slide outline

Voice: Daniel, a calm male macOS voice for a 9th-grade audience. Each numbered narration paragraph matches one slide.

1. Show a company hierarchy and circle two employees whose common boss is requested.
2. Contrast a slow parent-by-parent climb with powers-of-two elevator arrows.
3. Build the 1-boss, 2-boss, 4-boss, and 8-boss table for one branch.
4. Place two queried employees on different depth rows and lift the deeper one.
5. Show the immediate ancestor case where leveling makes the employees equal.
6. Animate both employees taking the largest safe equal-sized jumps.
7. Walk through all three official queries and highlight answers 3, 1, and 1.
8. Join the table invariant and safe-jump invariant into a short proof.
9. Display the commented C++ structure and `O((N+Q) log N)` bound.
