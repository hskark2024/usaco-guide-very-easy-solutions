# Storyboard and slide outline

Voice: Daniel, a calm male macOS voice for a 9th-grade audience. Each numbered narration paragraph matches one slide.

1. Paint a tree with three colors and flag an equal-colored adjacent pair.
2. Root the tree and place three color counters beside every barn.
3. Compare an unrestricted `(1,1,1)` leaf with a fixed `(0,0,1)` leaf.
4. Select a red parent and combine only the child's blue and green counters.
5. Multiply compatible totals from several child branches.
6. Walk through the official four-barn star and count eight choices.
7. Overlay fixed-color checks and edge checks on the two DP stages.
8. Animate reverse traversal and summarize time and memory.
