# Storyboard and slide outline

Voice: Daniel, a calm male macOS voice for a 9th-grade audience. Each numbered narration paragraph matches one slide.

1. Show one tree and many faded search waves, then replace them with two shared passes.
2. Root the tree and animate blue downward arrows from leaves toward the root.
3. Put a gold arrow through a parent and into a sibling subtree.
4. At one parent, compare the upward arrow with a sideways sibling arrow.
5. Highlight the tallest branch, block it for its own child, and select the second-tallest.
6. Trace the official five-node sample and reveal its five answers.
7. Divide paths into a blue “stays below” box and a gold “crosses parent” box.
8. Animate vertex order backward, then forward, ending on `O(N)`.
