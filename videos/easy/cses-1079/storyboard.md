# Storyboard

1. **Hook — choose a team**: Circle three of five student icons and display `5 choose 3 = 10`.
2. **Factorial identity**: Split `a!` orderings into the selected and unselected internal orders that must be removed.
3. **Shared preprocessing**: Fill a factorial array from left to right through the largest query.
4. **Modular division problem**: Cross out an ordinary division sign and replace it with “multiply by inverse.”
5. **Fermat and fast power**: Show `x^(P-2)` and a compact square-and-multiply ladder.
6. **Backward inverse factorials**: Animate one known inverse at the right end filling the array toward zero.
7. **Query and complexity**: Highlight three array lookups, two modular multiplications, and the final bounds.
