# Storyboard and slide outline

Voice: Daniel, a calm male macOS voice for a 9th-grade audience. Each narration paragraph matches one slide.

1. Place the station and criminals on a number line with a car capacity badge.
2. Animate a car reaching the farthest left criminal and collecting closer ones while returning.
3. Bracket left criminals into groups and highlight indices `0, M, 2M, ...`.
4. Reflect the same brackets onto the right side.
5. Slide the station between adjacent criminals and show a straight cost segment.
6. Build the left-side formula from leader count and leader-position sum.
7. Mirror the formula in a right-to-left sweep.
8. Trace the small official sample across candidate station positions.
9. Add the side costs and double for round trips.
10. End with the linear-time complexity card and million-input scale.
