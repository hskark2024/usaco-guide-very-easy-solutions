# Storyboard

1. **Repeated shuffling.** Show elements following fixed destination arrows.
2. **Permutation graph.** Rearrange arrows into separate rings.
3. **One cycle's clock.** A length-three ring resets every three rounds.
4. **Synchronize clocks.** Lengths three and four first align at twelve.
5. **The modulo trap.** Explain why reducing a partial LCM loses gcd information.
6. **Prime exponent solution.** Merge factorizations by maximum exponents.
7. **C++ traversal.** Walk cycles, factor lengths, and multiply the result.
