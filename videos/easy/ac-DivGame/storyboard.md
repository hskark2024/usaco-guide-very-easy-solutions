# Storyboard

1. **Hook — many possible divisions**: Show several prime powers branching from one number.
2. **Factor 24**: Separate the exponent supplies for `2^3` and `3^1`.
3. **Power costs**: Label `p^1,p^2,p^3` with costs `1,2,3`.
4. **Greedy staircase**: Pack the smallest distinct costs into exponent `e`.
5. **Proof**: Show that any `k` distinct positive costs sum to at least the kth triangular number.
6. **Examples and leftovers**: Trace 64, 1, a prime, and an exponent with unused copies.
7. **C++ factorization**: Walk through trial division, exponent counting, and the leftover-prime check.
