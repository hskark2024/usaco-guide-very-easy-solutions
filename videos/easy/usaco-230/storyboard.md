# Storyboard and slide outline

Voice: Daniel, a calm male macOS voice for a 9th-grade audience. Each narration paragraph matches one slide.

1. Introduce the map symbols and minimization goal.
2. Flood-fill land into numbered islands.
3. Compress the grid into a weighted graph.
4. Explain zero-cost land and one-cost shallow water.
5. Animate the 0-1 BFS deque rule.
6. Fill the pairwise island-cost table.
7. Define the subset DP state.
8. Initialize every possible starting island for free.
9. Connect shortest paths with visit-order enumeration.
10. Close with bounds, complexity, and tests.
