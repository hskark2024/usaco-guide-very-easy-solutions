# Edge-case checklist

- Proper interior crossing.
- Shared endpoint but different supporting lines.
- Parallel noncollinear segments.
- Collinear disjoint, touching, partially overlapping, and identical segments.
- Horizontal and vertical overlaps.
- Reversed endpoint order.
- One or both segments reduced to points.
- Fractional and negative-zero intersection coordinates.
- Wide integer cross products before any floating-point work.
