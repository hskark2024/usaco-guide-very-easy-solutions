# Test-case walkthroughs

For the official paths, the progress-grid route sometimes advances one traveler alone, letting the other wait. Adding the squared separations at every reached state gives the minimum total 28.

If both start together and follow the same one-step direction simultaneously, the diagonal transition keeps their distance zero and the answer is zero. If their route lengths differ, the shorter route may finish first while the other continues through horizontal or vertical grid edges.
