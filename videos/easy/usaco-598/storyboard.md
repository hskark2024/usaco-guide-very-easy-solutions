Voice: Daniel, a calm male macOS voice for a 9th-grade audience. Each numbered narration paragraph matches one slide.

1. Animate two walkers with fixed colored routes and the three timing choices.
2. Mark indexed prefix positions `F[0..N]` and `B[0..M]` on their routes.
3. Transform the route picture into a progress grid from `(0,0)` to `(N,M)`.
4. Draw horizontal, vertical, and diagonal incoming edges to one state.
5. Label the destination with squared separation and compare three predecessor totals.
6. Put a zero-cost badge only on the start and a charge badge after every edge.
7. Trace the official sample's cheapest grid path and reveal total 28.
8. Display rolling rows, complexity, and exhaustive schedule verification.
