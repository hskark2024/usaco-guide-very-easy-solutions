# Storyboard

1. **Hook — mixed letter tiles**: Rearrange `A A B A C` and ask why many swaps look identical.
2. **Pretend copies are labeled**: Turn the three A tiles into A1, A2, A3 and show `5!` labeled orders.
3. **Remove duplicate labels**: Group the six A-label orders that collapse into one visible string.
4. **Multiset formula**: Display `n! / product(count!)` next to the 20 arrangements of `aabac`.
5. **Modular division**: Replace the denominator with its modular inverse using Fermat's theorem.
6. **C++ data flow**: Highlight the frequency array, factorial loop, denominator, and fast power.
7. **Proof and limits**: Summarize one-to-one counting, `O(n + log P)` time, and key edge cases.
