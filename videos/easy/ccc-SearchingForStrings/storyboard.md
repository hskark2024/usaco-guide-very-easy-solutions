# Storyboard and slide outline

Voice: Daniel, a calm male macOS voice for a 9th-grade audience. Each narration paragraph matches one slide.

1. Introduce the needle, haystack, and distinct-permutation goal.
2. Show why only equal-length windows can qualify.
3. Place 26 letter counters under a moving window.
4. Explain the remove-one, add-one sliding update.
5. Contrast an order-free frequency table with an order-sensitive hash.
6. Draw two prefix-hash rulers and one substring subtraction.
7. Walk through `aab` in `abacabaa`.
8. Put repeated `aba` fingerprints into one set bucket.
9. Present the full pipeline and the too-long early exit.
10. End with complexity, double-hash safety, and testing.
