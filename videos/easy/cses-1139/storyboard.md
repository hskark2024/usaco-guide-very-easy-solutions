# Storyboard and slide outline

Voice: Daniel, a calm male macOS voice for a 9th-grade audience. Each numbered narration paragraph matches one slide.

1. Show repeated colors across a rooted tree and faded duplicate set-building work.
2. Animate preorder flattening, matching subtrees to colored array brackets.
3. Replace the tree with a stack of interval questions over one array.
4. Sweep right to left and show one glowing marker per visible color.
5. Encounter a repeated color, erase its old marker, and move it left.
6. Stop at a subtree entry and total markers inside its bracket.
7. Trace the official five-node sample and reveal `3 1 2 1 1`.
8. Show three copies of one color collapsing into exactly one marker.
9. Show explicit DFS events, Fenwick operations, and final complexity.
