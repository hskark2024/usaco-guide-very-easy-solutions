# Storyboard

1. **Hook — hand out apples**: Three labeled children receive two identical apple icons in several arrangements.
2. **Star encoding**: Replace each apple icon with `*`.
3. **Divider encoding**: Insert two bars and label the three resulting sections by child.
4. **Zero apples**: Animate adjacent bars and bars at both ends to show empty sections.
5. **Bijection**: Arrows run from a distribution to a stars-and-bars row and back.
6. **Combination formula**: Count `m+n-1` positions and highlight the chosen `n-1` bar slots.
7. **Code and bounds**: Show factorial construction, modular inverse, official `C(4,2)=6`, and complexity.
