# Storyboard

1. Present the three command cards beside an array.
2. Collapse a long updated interval into one lazy segment-tree node.
3. Show a `set x` stamp replacing the node's old tags and sum.
4. Show an `add y` badge increasing the node sum.
5. Merge `set x` followed by `add y` into `set x+y`.
6. Animate a parent pushing assignment first and addition second to two children.
7. Recurse through a partially covered range and pull child sums upward.
8. Walk through the sample answers `7`, `11`, and `15`.
9. Match each tag-composition rule to the chronological array operations.
10. Finish with complexity and random plus maximum-operation verification.
