# Storyboard and slide outline

Voice: Daniel, a calm male macOS voice for a 9th-grade audience. Each numbered narration paragraph matches one slide.

1. Show edges being deleted until each remaining component is fully connected.
2. Circle complete vertex groups and label them cliques.
3. Turn vertex subsets into on/off bit rows.
4. Build a clique mask by adding one vertex to a smaller clique.
5. Place a `dp[mask]` card over one full clique.
6. Anchor the lowest vertex and highlight candidate clique submasks containing it.
7. Compare repeated group orderings and cross out the symmetric copies.
8. Display complexity and the independent partition verifier.
