# Storyboard and slide outline

Voice: Daniel, a calm male macOS voice for a 9th-grade audience. Each numbered narration paragraph matches one slide.

1. Highlight two disjoint edges in a tree and fade an illegal pair sharing a vertex.
2. Root the tree and place `blocked` and `best` cards beside each vertex.
3. Collapse child subtrees into their `best` values and add them.
4. Light one parent-child edge and swap that child's card to `blocked`.
5. Attempt two child edges, mark the shared parent red, then separate the choices.
6. Trace the two chosen edges in the official sample.
7. Show the exhaustive fork: no child edge versus one child edge.
8. Animate iterative order forward and DP evaluation backward.
