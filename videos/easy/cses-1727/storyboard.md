# Storyboard

1. **Hook — random candy cards**: Two children reveal cards numbered one through three; circle the larger card.
2. **Outcome table**: Show the nine pairs for the official example and their maxima summing to 22.
3. **Threshold stack**: Build a height-four column from four reached levels.
4. **Tail-sum identity**: Replace the height with indicators `max >= 1`, `max >= 2`, and so on.
5. **Complement event**: Move all child markers below a chosen threshold `x`.
6. **Independence**: Multiply `((x-1)/k)` once for each child, then subtract from one.
7. **Code and precision**: Show the `long double` loop, six-decimal output, and `O(k)` bound.
