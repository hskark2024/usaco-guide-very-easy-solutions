# Storyboard and slide outline

Voice: Daniel, a calm male macOS voice for a 9th-grade audience. Each numbered narration paragraph matches one slide.

1. Show a blank one-dimensional canvas and several interval-shaped brush strokes.
2. Layer a long blue stroke under two shorter strokes, leaving blue visible at both ends.
3. Highlight intervals in a triangular DP table and define `dp[left][right]`.
4. Isolate the left cell as one separate stroke, then bracket the suffix.
5. Connect two equal-colored cells with one extended underlying stroke.
6. Color the middle and suffix as separate subproblems and reveal the formula.
7. Build the official sample in six colored layers.
8. Show the two exhaustive cases for the left cell's final-color stroke.
9. Count states, transitions, time, and memory.
