# Storyboard

1. **Hook — too many trial divisions**: Show 100,000 queries making the direct method explode.
2. **Factor 72**: Animate `72 = 2^3 * 3^2`.
3. **Exponent choices**: Build a four-by-three grid of divisor combinations.
4. **Derive the formula**: Generalize to the product of `(ei+1)`.
5. **Smallest-prime-factor sieve**: Label every number through the maximum query.
6. **Query walkthrough**: Divide 72 by two three times and three twice.
7. **Edge cases and code**: Explain one, primes, complexity, and C++ structure.
