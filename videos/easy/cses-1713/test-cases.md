# Test-case walkthroughs

## Official value: `16`

`16 = 2^4`, so its divisors choose one of five exponents and the answer is `5`.

## Official prime: `17`

`17 = 17^1`, giving `1+1 = 2` divisors.

## Official value: `18`

`18 = 2^1 * 3^2`, so the answer is `(1+1)(2+1)=6`.

## Identity value: `1`

The factorization is empty. The product stays `1`, accounting for divisor one.

## Maximum-style composite: `1,000,000`

`1,000,000 = 2^6 * 5^6`, so the divisor count is `7*7=49`.
