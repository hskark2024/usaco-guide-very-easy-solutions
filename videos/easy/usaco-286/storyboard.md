# Storyboard

1. Show a two-colored tree and define a balanced rest-stop path.
2. Replace the colors with `+1` and `-1` edge labels.
3. Highlight a centroid and separate its neighbor branches by color.
4. Write prefix-balance labels beside vertices in two branches.
5. Pair opposite endpoint labels to make the whole path sum zero.
6. Highlight two equal labels on one root path and the zero-sum section between them.
7. Animate total-label and unusable-label histograms as branches merge.
8. Remove the centroid, recurse, and finish with uniqueness and complexity.
