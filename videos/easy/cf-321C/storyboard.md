# Storyboard

1. Present ranks `A` through `Z` and the equal-rank path condition.
2. Introduce a centroid as a balanced separator.
3. Label the whole-tree centroid `A` and remove it.
4. Label each remaining component's centroid `B`.
5. Continue the decomposition with `C`, `D`, and later ranks.
6. Prove that equal-rank vertices are separated by an earlier centroid.
7. Walk through a star and a chain.
8. Summarize halving depth and `O(N log N)` time.
