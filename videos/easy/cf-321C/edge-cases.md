# Edge-case checklist

- A single vertex.
- Two vertices with valid `A B` labeling.
- Star centered at vertex `1`.
- Star centered away from vertex `1`.
- Odd- and even-length chains.
- Two possible centroids in an even component.
- Highly unbalanced tree.
- Maximum-size chain without recursive DFS.
- Every produced rank remains between `A` and `Z`.
