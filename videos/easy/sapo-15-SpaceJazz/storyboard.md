# Storyboard and slide outline

Voice: Daniel, a calm male macOS voice for a 9th-grade audience. Each numbered narration paragraph matches one slide.

1. Animate doubled letter tiles being inserted into a growing melody.
2. Show adjacent equal pairs disappearing, then redraw the same structure as nested arcs.
3. Highlight a square interval in a triangular DP table and define its meaning.
4. Place a new copy next to the leftmost note and fade that pair away.
5. Connect the leftmost note to a later equal note; color the inside and suffix differently.
6. Put the full recurrence on screen one term at a time.
7. Walk through `aagog`, pairing `aa`, then the two `g` notes, then inserting one `o`.
8. Split all possible optimal completions into the two first-note cases.
9. Count `O(N^2)` states and `O(N)` partner trials per state.
