# Storyboard and slide outline

Voice: Daniel, a calm male macOS voice for a 9th-grade audience. Each narration paragraph matches one slide.

1. Introduce one-to-one assignments and prefix bonuses.
2. Compare factorial search with subset DP.
3. Explain what a mask and its bit count mean.
4. Define the best-score DP state.
5. Show one unused-cow transition.
6. Place bonuses at the correct prefix.
7. Demonstrate same-prefix bonus chaining.
8. Explain why lower-scoring duplicate states can be discarded.
9. Walk through the official sample assignment.
10. Close with complexity and independent verification.
