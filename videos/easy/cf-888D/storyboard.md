# Storyboard

1. **Hook — numbered cards and slots**: Show the identity permutation with every card in its matching home.
2. **Exactly two changes**: Circle two slots and swap their cards.
3. **The one-change trap**: Demonstrate why exactly one mismatch is impossible in a permutation.
4. **Choose then derange**: Split the count into `C(n,m)` selected slots and `D(m)` legal shuffles.
5. **Tiny derangement table**: Animate the values `1, 0, 1, 2, 9` for sizes zero through four.
6. **Worked example**: Add the `m=0`, `m=2`, and `m=3` terms for `n=5, k=3` to reach 31.
7. **C++ and proof**: Highlight exact combinations, 64-bit totals, disjoint groups, and constant-size work.
