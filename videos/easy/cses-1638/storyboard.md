# Storyboard and slide outline

Voice: Daniel, a calm male macOS voice for a 9th-grade audience. Each numbered narration paragraph matches one slide.

1. Introduce the grid, endpoints, legal directions, traps, and modulus. Show a small board with right/down arrows.
2. Zoom into one open cell with two incoming arrows labeled above and left.
3. Contrast a green starting cell carrying 1 with a trapped cell carrying 0.
4. Animate a one-row strip; show an entry changing from the old row to the new row.
5. Fill the official sample grid in sweep order and highlight the final 3.
6. Show a red “stale value” crossing a trap, then erase it when the entry is reset.
7. Display the time and memory bounds beside a 1000×1000 grid.
8. Close with the final-move proof and a checklist of independent tests.
