# Edge-case checklist

- `N=1` with repeated toggles.
- Initially empty and initially full grids.
- Single-cell rectangles.
- Full-grid rectangle.
- Rectangles touching the top or left edge.
- Rectangles touching the bottom or right edge.
- Repeated toggles of the same square.
- Queries before any update and after many updates.
- Official maximum `N=1000` and `Q=200,000`.
