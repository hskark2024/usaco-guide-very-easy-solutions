# Storyboard and slide outline

Voice: Daniel, a calm male macOS voice for a 9th-grade audience. Each narration paragraph matches one slide.

1. Contrast `2^40` with the feasible goal.
2. Split forty values into two halves.
3. Build subset sums by doubling a list.
4. Pair one left subset with one right subset.
5. Sort and search for the complement.
6. Count the complete equal range.
7. Explain why duplicate sums stay.
8. Walk through the official sample.
9. State the one-to-one correctness argument.
10. Close with complexity and verification.
