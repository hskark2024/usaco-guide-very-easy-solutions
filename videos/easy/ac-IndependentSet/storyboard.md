# Storyboard and slide outline

Voice: Daniel, a calm male macOS voice for a 9th-grade audience. Each numbered narration paragraph matches one slide.

1. Color a sample tree and flash an invalid black-black edge.
2. Root the tree and show white/black counters at each vertex.
3. From a white parent, draw arrows to both child colors and a plus sign.
4. From a black parent, keep only the arrow to white.
5. Start at leaf cards `(1,1)` and combine upward to the root.
6. Display all eight colorings of a three-node path and cross out three.
7. Separate the tree into independent child branches after fixing the parent.
8. Animate the iterative order and show the linear complexity tally.
