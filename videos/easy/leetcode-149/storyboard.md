1. Show several dots with one visibly crowded line.
2. Highlight one anchor and draw vectors to all other points.
3. Compare scaled vectors `(2,3)` and `(4,6)`.
4. Divide both vectors by their coordinate GCD.
5. Flip opposite vectors into one canonical sign.
6. Group vertical, horizontal, positive, and negative slope keys.
7. Count the largest group and add the anchor.
8. Review correctness, complexity, and integer safety.
