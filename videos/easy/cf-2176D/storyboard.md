# Storyboard and slide outline

Voice: Daniel, a calm male macOS voice for a 9th-grade audience. Each narration paragraph matches one slide.

1. Introduce graph labels and the Fibonacci rule.
2. Highlight that every single edge is valid.
3. Draw `u -> v -> w` and the required value at `w`.
4. Group outgoing paths by their second value.
5. Build the edge transition with one lookup.
6. Show a graph cycle and the apparent dependency problem.
7. Order edges by decreasing endpoint sum.
8. Update one map bucket and the answer.
9. Explain why positive growth prevents repeated vertices.
10. Close with brute-force and scale verification.
