# Storyboard and slide outline

Voice: Daniel, a calm male macOS voice for a 9th-grade audience. Each numbered narration paragraph matches one slide.

1. Show cows stacked under a frisbee, with height, weight, and strength labels.
2. Highlight spare strength on each cow and circle the smallest margin.
3. Cross out simple sorting rules, then display a 20-bit subset mask.
4. Define `dp[mask]` beside the infinity-valued empty state.
5. Remove the top cow from a three-cow stack and clear her bit.
6. Animate the top cow's weight reducing the lower safety margin.
7. Fan out all top-cow choices, then filter subsets by total height.
8. Show sample answer two, failure text, and complexity bounds.
