# Storyboard and slide outline

Voice: Daniel, a calm male macOS voice for a 9th-grade audience. Each numbered narration paragraph matches one slide.

1. Show two columns of people connected only by allowed green edges.
2. Contrast a huge permutation tree with merged subset states.
3. Display a bit row such as `01011` and count its set bits.
4. Label `dp[mask]` and highlight the empty-mask base case.
5. Animate one unused compatible bit turning on.
6. Trace one finished matching through a unique sequence of masks.
7. Walk through the three-by-three sample and two extreme matrices.
8. Show the `N * 2^N` time and `2^N` memory bounds.
