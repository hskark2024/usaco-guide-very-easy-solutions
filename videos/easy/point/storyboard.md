# Storyboard

1. Place `P1`, `P2`, and `P3` on a coordinate grid.
2. Draw the directed viewing arrow `P1→P2`.
3. Show example points in the left and right half-planes.
4. Translate both arrows to a common origin at `P1`.
5. Build the cross-product formula from the two vector coordinates.
6. Map positive, negative, and zero signs to the three outputs.
7. Walk through all three official sample queries.
8. Close with overflow safety and `O(T)` complexity.
