# Storyboard and slide outline

Voice: Daniel, a calm male macOS voice for a 9th-grade audience. Each narration paragraph matches one slide.

1. Show a rooftop, a power-up timer, and a faster falling path.
2. Build the formula from setup time and fall time.
3. Overlay the rising line, falling curve, and convex total.
4. Compare doing nothing with power-up cost beyond `A/B`.
5. Animate two ternary probes and discard one outer third.
6. Zoom into the final integer candidates and check all of them.
7. Walk through operation counts zero through three for `A=10, B=1`.
8. Show why `A=5, B=10` chooses zero operations.
9. Highlight `long double`, `sqrtl`, and precise output.
10. End with logarithmic time, constant space, and the convex-search pattern.
