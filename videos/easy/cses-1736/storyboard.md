# Storyboard

1. Show an array with a `+1,+2,+3,...` ribbon across a selected interval.
2. Animate a per-cell loop growing too large, then replace it with a tree.
3. Label the update start `l` and a general position `i`.
4. Rearrange `i-l+1` into slope and intercept color blocks.
5. Zoom out from cells to one segment-tree interval and total its line.
6. Display the arithmetic-series formula for absolute indices.
7. Put slope and intercept into a lazy-tag card and merge two cards.
8. Walk from sample total `17` to `32` by adding the triangular sum `15`.
9. Connect per-position correctness, node sums, and lazy pushing.
10. Finish with complexity and random plus maximum-size verification.
